# Red Team Review

Score a proposal draft as a government evaluator would, identifying strengths, weaknesses, deficiencies, and providing actionable fix recommendations.

## Workflow

1. Identify what to review — a specific section file, the full proposal, or text provided in conversation
2. Read Section M evaluation criteria from the solicitation analysis (`analysis.md`)
3. For each evaluation factor/subfactor:
   - Score using the solicitation's rating scale (Outstanding/Good/Acceptable/Marginal/Unacceptable or Blue/Purple/Green/Yellow/Red)
   - Identify **Strengths**: Aspects that exceed requirements or increase confidence
   - Identify **Weaknesses**: Flaws that increase risk of unsuccessful performance
   - Identify **Deficiencies**: Material failures to meet requirements
   - Write evaluator-style notes with specific references to the proposal text
4. Provide a specific **fix recommendation** for every weakness and deficiency
5. Run compliance checks: all Section L requirements addressed? Page limits met? Win themes present? Format correct?
6. Present priority actions ranked by impact

## Review Types

The user can request specific review levels:
- **Pink Team**: Direction and coverage check (50-75% draft)
- **Red Team**: Full evaluator scoring (complete draft) — this is the default
- **Gold Team**: Executive review for strategic messaging
- **White Glove**: Compliance and formatting only
